# Code of conduct

NarraReel is for creators and developers of every background. We want a project where people ask questions without
fear and disagree without hostility.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be welcoming to newcomers. Explain, don't gatekeep.
- Keep discussion on topic and respect each other's time.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal information or voice recordings without permission.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, personal attacks, or threats.

## Voice cloning

Because this project can clone voices: do not use NarraReel, or ask for help, to impersonate someone, mislead
people, or imitate a voice without the owner's permission. Issues and pull requests that aim to remove the
consent step or the audio watermark will be closed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
people who repeatedly or seriously do. To report a problem, email the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile
of the repository owner, or open a private security advisory on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1.
